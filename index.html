A day at Happy Kindergarten

Predictable routines that help children feel safe, calm, and ready to explore.

8:00
Soft arrival & free play Children choose activities, greet friends, and settle in at their own pace.
9:30
Circle time & small groups Songs, stories, and focused activities tailored to each age group.
10:15
Outdoor play Yard time, nature walks, and movement games in fresh air.
11:30
Lunch & calm time Warm meals, followed by rest, reading, or quiet play.
13:00
Afternoon projects Art, building, role play, and child-led projects.
15:00
Snack & pick-up window Gentle transitions home with time for a last play.

Note: Times are flexible—families can choose arrival and pick-up within our opening hours.
